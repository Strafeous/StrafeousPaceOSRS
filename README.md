# Strafeous' Pace

A RuneLite skilling pace timer and session logger for Old School RuneScape.

## Features
* **Instant Action Start:** Starts tracking at `00:00` the exact tick your character begins a skilling animation (swinging a pickaxe, chopping a tree, casting a fishing rod/net, or laying/checking a hunter trap) or gains XP.
* **Smart 120-Second AFK Protection:** Automatically stops counting idle time and saves your active run if you go AFK for more than 120 seconds (configurable in settings).
* **Automatic Session Logging:** Automatically saves your completed run to the sidebar panel and a local log file (`~/.runelite/strafeous-pace/strafeous-pace-log.txt`) on AFK timeout, world hop, logout, client close, or switching to a different skill.
* **Comprehensive Live & Saved Stats:**
  * Total items gathered & **items/hr** (smart-labeled for ore, logs, fish, catches, bars, and laps)
  * Total XP gained, **XP/hr**, and **average XP per item**
  * **Seconds per item** pace and optional **Goal ETA**
  * Total Grand Exchange loot value and **GP/hr**
  * Automatic **skilling location detection** (Motherlode Mine, Mining Guild, Feldip Hills, Piscatoris, Woodcutting Guild, and more)
* **Visual Loot Grid & 1-Click Export:** Displays dated session cards with item icons in the right-hand sidebar panel and includes an **Export Log** button to generate a formatted `.txt` report on your Desktop.

## Controls
* **Move Overlay:** Hold `Alt` and drag the on-screen timer box anywhere on your screen.
* **Quick Actions:** Right-click the on-screen timer box to **Save & Reset**, **Pause/Resume**, or **Reset**.
