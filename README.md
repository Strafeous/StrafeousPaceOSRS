Strafeous' Pace

A simple RuneLite timer and session logger for tracking your skilling pace in Old School RuneScape.

What It Does

Strafeous' Pace starts tracking the exact tick you begin skilling—whether you are hunting, mining, woodcutting, fishing, crafting, or training other skills. Instead of guessing your rates or manually starting a stopwatch, the timer kicks off automatically as soon as your character begins an action or gains XP.
If you step away from your keyboard, log out, hop worlds, or switch to another skill, the plugin automatically pauses idle time and saves your completed session so your hourly rates stay accurate.

Tracked Stats and Features

• Automatic Start and AFK Protection: Starts on your first skilling action and automatically stops counting idle time after 120 seconds of inactivity (configurable in settings).
• Live Pace and Rates: Tracks total drops and items gathered per hour, total XP and XP/hr, average XP per item, seconds per item, Grand Exchange loot value, GP/hr, and an optional goal ETA.
• Location Detection: Automatically recognizes popular skilling spots like Motherlode Mine, Mining Guild, Feldip Hills, Piscatoris, and the Woodcutting Guild.
• Session History and Export: Saves dated session cards with visual loot icons in the sidebar panel, alongside a one-click Export Log button that saves a formatted text report to your RuneLite plugin data folder.

Controls

• Move Overlay: Hold Alt and drag the timer box anywhere on your screen.
• Quick Actions: Right-click the timer box to Save & Reset, Pause/Resume, or Reset.
